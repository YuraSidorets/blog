## Interesting Blogs I Read

This list contains blogs I find particularly interesting and useful. They cover various topics from technology to psychology and the art of thinking.

### Technology & Development

#### [Paul Graham Essays](https://paulgraham.com/articles.html)
- **Why read it**: Deep thoughts on startups, technology, and education
- **Key topics**: Startups, programming, education, technology
- **Style**: Clear, insightful, without unnecessary jargon

#### [Joel on Software](https://www.joelonsoftware.com/)
- **Why read it**: Classic articles on software development and project management
- **Key topics**: Software development, management, IT careers
- **Style**: Witty, practical, with rich experience

#### [Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/)
- **Why read it**: Best articles and discussions about technology
- **Key topics**: Startups, technology, careers, innovations
- **Style**: Community, diverse perspectives

### Psychology & Cognitive Science

#### [LessWrong](https://www.lesswrong.com/)

- **Key topics**: Rationality, cognitive biases, philosophy

#### [Wait But Why](https://waitbutwhy.com/)

- **Key topics**: Science, technology, human nature
- **Style**: Visually rich, beginner-friendly

#### [Overcoming Bias](https://www.overcomingbias.com/)

- **Key topics**: Cognitive science, psychology, economics

## How I Choose Blogs

I evaluate blogs based on these criteria:

1. **Content depth** - not superficial articles, but analysis and research
2. **Writing quality** - clear style, good structure, interesting presentation
3. **Relevance** - fresh thoughts and research
4. **Long-term value** - content that remains useful for years
5. **Originality** - unique perspectives and analysis
